# Sleepy

A workspace for exploring a business around the founder's own experience with CPAP and sleep data.

**Stage:** business discovery and technical feasibility. No application, device connector, or integration has been implemented in this repository.

## Open the Obsidian vault

1. In Obsidian, choose **Open folder as vault**.
2. Select /home/user/Desktop/all/sleepy/vault.
3. Open [Home](vault/Home.md).

The vault includes the supplied ChatGPT handoff, customer discovery materials, business hypotheses, product options, data-access feasibility, decisions, experiments, and templates. It uses standard Markdown and built-in Obsidian features; no community plugins are required.

## Business development plan

Start with [Business development plan](vault/Business/Business%20development%20plan.md). It defines the future research, feasibility, commercial testing, pilot, launch, and expansion activities, with deliverables and investment gates. Market research is planned, not executed. The schedule, targets, and budgets remain proposals.

## First question

What specific, recurring problem does the founder experience with myAir or managing CPAP sleep? Capture one real example in [Founder problem](vault/Business/Founder%20problem.md), then test whether other people share it.

## Workspace layout

- vault/: linked business and project knowledge, including Obsidian configuration.
- AGENTS.md: context and working rules for future development agents.
- private/: ignored local material such as personal health exports, device logs, and identifiable interview notes. Create subfolders here when needed.

Keep shared research anonymized. Do not add health exports, credentials, or identifiable participant records to Git. Ignore rules help prevent accidental additions; they do not encrypt files or remove previously tracked data.

The initial vault setup was published to main on 2026-11-11 as commit 5a7db73. Current business activity is planning; no customer outreach, device integration, application deployment, or paid pilot has been performed.
