# Open questions

## Business first

1. What specific recurring problem does the founder experience? See [[Founder problem]].
2. Who else experiences it, how often, and what do they currently do?
3. What alternatives already solve it?
4. Who would pay for which outcome, with what evidence of commitment?
5. Side business or larger startup? What time and investment constraints apply?

## Data feasibility

- AirSense 11 firmware/region and Ubuntu/Bluetooth environment?
- Authorized read-only BLE therapy-data interface for this setup?
- SD card/reader available, and what does permitted data support?
- Watch worn overnight? Which Watch, iPhone, software versions, and history?
- Sleep Cycle on iPhone? What exports or syncs for this installation?
- Which sources overlap? How do timestamps, units, and definitions differ?

Ask hardware details when needed for the next experiment rather than collecting everything before defining the problem.

## Questions before commercialization

These need current research and, where appropriate, qualified assessment. No legal conclusions have been reached.

- Data-access and commercial-use rights for selected routes?
- Intended purpose/product claims and resulting EU/Swedish obligations?
- Health-data processing, consent or other lawful basis, retention, deletion/export, and controller/processor arrangements?
- What would customers need to trust data handling and explanations?
- Licensing obligations for reused parsers and other code?

Track findings in [[Evidence register]] and decisions in [[Decision log]].
