# Product brief

## Working concept

Sleepy may become a personal web application helping people explore sleep and CPAP data. The earlier concept combines AirSense 11, Apple Watch/Apple Health, and Sleep Cycle records. This direction comes from [[2026-11-11 - ChatGPT handoff]] and is not a validated product requirement.

## Customer and problem

The founder is the starting research participant. The customer segment and recurring problem remain open: [[Founder problem]] and [[Business model hypotheses]].

## Possible outcomes to investigate

- Understand a specific pattern across nights.
- See relevant measurements from multiple tools in a time-aligned view.
- Prepare a source-attributed report of personal observations.

These are hypotheses. Choose one based on evidence rather than implementing them all.

## Boundaries proposed in the handoff

Ground explanations in available measurements, retain uncertainty, and distinguish association from causation. Do not make unsupported diagnostic or treatment recommendations. Automated therapy-setting changes are outside the described concept.

No working connectors, validated access, usability results, interviews, price, or commercial classification exists in this project. See [[Data source feasibility]], [[MVP options]], and [[Open questions]].
