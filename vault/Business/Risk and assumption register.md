# Risk and assumption register

Initial risks to investigate, not findings that the business is infeasible. Likelihood and quantified financial impact are unknown. Priorities indicate the order in which uncertainty should be reduced.

| ID | Assumption / risk | Priority | Owner accountable | Next evidence | Gate affected |

| R03 | Existing alternatives leave a meaningful gap | First | Founder | Same-task alternative comparison | 1 |
| R04 | The buyer will pay enough for the outcome | High | Founder | Current spending and response to a concrete offer | 3, 6 |

## Review method

For each priority risk, record:
- Current evidence and confidence.
- Consequence if the assumption is false.
- Cheapest appropriate next test.
- Cash/hour ceiling, accountable person, review date.
- Result, counterevidence, and the resulting investment decision.

Use [[Experiment]] for tests and link to [[Evidence register]]. A risk is resolved only for the scope actually assessed. A personal-device success does not establish commercial compatibility across all customer setups.

Review weekly and at every gate in [[Business development plan]]. New scope, geography, customer type, data source, or product claim can reopen previously reviewed risks.
