# Customer discovery

## Objective

Understand whether a specific problem from [[Founder problem]] recurs for others, what they do about it, and what improvement would be useful.

**Completed interviews: 0.** No participants have been contacted through this project.

## Proposed approach

Review what we learn after five conversations, then decide whether to continue toward the handoff's suggested 10–20. These counts are planning choices, not proof of demand. Include experiences and workarounds different from the founder's.

Ask about recent behavior before describing Sleepy or integrations.

## Interview guide

1. Tell me about the last time you wanted to understand something about your sleep or CPAP use.
2. What triggered the question? What did you do next?
3. Which tools or people did you turn to? What worked, and where did you get stuck?
4. How often has that happened recently?
5. What did the situation or workaround cost in time, effort, or money?
6. What have you tried already? Have you paid for anything to help?
7. What would a useful result let you do?
8. If a product needed your sleep data, what would you need to understand before considering it?

After the problem discussion, show a narrow concept and explore its usefulness compared with the current alternative. A positive reaction is not a purchase commitment.
